## Visual review

1. Crop/flower shop: mirrored-side house source (House 3) instead of repeating House 2. Sales veranda and warm pale plaster/wood. Still retains a tall gable and upstairs window; requested low one-storey form was not fully achieved. Flower/seed cues are subtler than the previous shop. Roof pictogram is ambiguous.
2. Perennial nursery: cropped one 96x128 source frame from a greenhouse atlas, no resizing. Glass partly replaced by timber/bamboo slats, brick footings and potted saplings. Distinct narrow open structure, but its tall front-heavy appearance and limited side-depth need in-game camera review.
3. Farm-tool workshop: Blacksmith source, dark timber, blue-green tile, stone base and red-brick furnace. Large work bay and tools make a separate industrial silhouette. Opening reads overly dark/empty; not every requested tool is clearly identifiable.
4. Produce warehouse: barn source, timber plank facade, open produce racks and stone loading apron. Function and material clearer; requested wide/low mass remains dominated by tall barn roof.
5. Seed store: shed source transformed to ivory plaster and red-brick footing with blue-green roof. Strong material distinction, but rotated toward an oblique/isometric-looking view and did not keep source facade orientation. Door closed and sacks not clearly displayed. Candidate, not camera-approved.
6. Restaurant: wider House 5 stone source, plastered facade, sheltered seating wing and open cooking counter with brick hearth. Broad silhouette differs from other shops. Tall central gable remains and the hearth dominates; requested bowl sign is not clearly present. A detached kettle outside the silhouette was removed using a free exact PixelLab workbench edit (120-pixel component), without a further generation charge.

No obvious lettering observed; icon clarity is mixed. Native sprite canvases verified; no in-engine camera, lighting, doorway size or collision validation. This set offers more varied source geometry and materials, not six production-approved buildings. Best next review targets: seed-store perspective, nursery depth, and brighter workshop interior.
